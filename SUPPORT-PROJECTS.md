Session estimates assume approximately 45–60 minutes and are planning estimates, not deadlines. Finish one assignment and record its evidence before taking on the next.

## What every completed project needs

- A business request and a short explanation of your design.
- Configuration or scripts with fictional data.
- Expected results and actual positive/negative tests.
- Redacted screenshots or command output.
- A problem you investigated, the cause you established and a verified fix.
- A short README describing completed scope, remaining limitations and lessons learned.

## Working method

For each session: read one request, write what you would check, perform the work, capture evidence, then explain the outcome in your own words. Ask for a hint when stuck. Keep a known-working baseline before introducing reversible faults.
